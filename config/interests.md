## SCORING RUBRIC

Score every candidate once, in one pass, then sort. Total is 0-100. Five components, one multiplier set,
two penalties. Nothing here needs a second look at the item.

| Component | Range | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **T — tier / source weight** | 0-30 | YouTube tier 1 = 30, tier 2 = 20, tier 3 = 10. News = 8 x feed weight (weight 3 -> 24, 2 -> 16, 1 -> 8). |
| **F — freshness** | 0-15 | published < 6h = 15, < 12h = 12, < 18h = 8, < 30h = 5, < 50h = 3, older = 0. The window is 50 hours because the run is every second day, so a two-day-old story still scores — it is simply worth less than this morning's. |
| **N — novelty vs memory** | -25..15 | never covered = 15; last covered > 60 days = 12; 21-60 days = 6; inside 21 days = -25. |
| **V — format value** | 0-25 | See format table below. |
| **M — personal multiplier** | -15..15 | LOVES topic = +15, LIKES = +8, NEUTRAL = 0, DISLIKES = -15, NEVER = excluded entirely. |

Format value table (V): a creator's take on a big event = 25; a trailer / reveal / first-look reaction = 22;
a documentary or long-form essay = 25; a review or analysis = 20; a plain news article = 15; a podcast or
interview clip = 10. **Gameplay and let's-play uploads are not scored at all** - CHANNEL POLICY
excludes them, so they never reach the ranking table. A stream VOD over 3 hours is likewise 0 and
never leads. The ordering is the point:
commentary and documentary outrank gameplay because that is what he respects (memory.md L1745-1747);
nothing in memory says he dislikes long gameplay, so it is ranked last rather than removed.

`score = T + F + N + V + M`, then penalties, then clamp to 0-100.

Penalties: **-45** if this exact item already shipped in any edition (memory says seen); **-45** if the same
topic shipped inside the cooldown window and there is no comeback exception. Apply each once.

Thresholds: **>= 80** lead candidate (highest wins, exactly one lead). **60-79** full story slot.
**45-59** quick hit or a short `quick` section. **< 45** drop. If the best item scores under 60, publish a
short honest edition anyway - five thin stories beat a blank page.

Worked example, so the numbers calibrate:

| Item | T | F | N | V | M | Penalty | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Asmongold reacts to a studio closure, 4h old, never covered | 30 (tier 1) | 15 | 15 | 25 (creator take on big event) | +15 (LOVES) | 0 | **100** (clamped) |
| PC Gamer, weight 2, Warframe update, 20h old, topic last covered 40d ago | 16 | 5 | 6 | 15 (article) | +8 (LIKES) | 0 | **50** |
| Tier-3 meme channel, 3h let's play of a game he does not play, 10h old | 10 | 12 | 15 | 0 (3h+ VOD) | 0 | 0 | **37** -> drop |
| Same routine let's play, but it shipped yesterday | 10 | 12 | -25 | 5 | 0 | -45 | **0** -> suppressed |

Read the table top to bottom: the 100 leads, the 50 becomes a story or quick hit, the 37 is dropped, and
the repeat is dead regardless of how good it is.

## TONE RULES

- **Funny first, never at someone's expense.** A joke about a delay, a price, a 400GB install or a studio's
  own hype is fine. A joke about a named private person, a layoff victim or a death is not.
- **Warm.** The register is a friend who read everything overnight and is pleased to tell you. Not a press
  release, not a cynic, not a hype account.
- **Optimistic, including on bad news.** Do: "Studios closed, but the two leads went indie and their first
  build is already playable." Don't: "The industry is collapsing and nothing will save it."
- **No filler.** No "in today's edition", no "let's dive in", no "it remains to be seen", no restating the
  headline in the standfirst.
- **No invented facts.** No estimated numbers, no "reportedly", no reconstructed quotes. If a number is not
  in the source, it is not in the sentence.
- **Headlines: sentence case, capital first word, no full stop.** Decisively not all-lowercase - lowercase
  headlines read as a style affectation, and he has no patience for posturing (memory.md L439-440).
- **Em dashes: maximum one per paragraph**, and never two in the same sentence. **Exclamation marks: zero
  in body copy, maximum one in the whole edition**, and only inside an aside.
- **The aside** is one sentence, ends the section, and is the only place a joke may be a stretch. Written
  in the same voice as the body, never labelled "joke:".
- **Standfirst: exactly one sentence, 12-30 words**, adds the fact the headline could not carry. Never a
  second headline, never a list.
- **Numbers in the body, attitude in the headline.** The headline is allowed to be funny; the body is not
  allowed to be vague.
- **Never cruel, never smug.** No "imagine caring about", no dunking on fans of a game he does not play.
- **Second person is allowed once per edition**, not once per section.

## COOLDOWN

- **Topic rule: 21 days.** `repeat_topic_cooldown_days` in `config/settings.json`. A topic covered inside
  that window does not get another slot, however big the new number is.
- **Same topic means the same story object**, not the same franchise: "Warframe 1999 update" and "Warframe
  hits a player-count record" are one topic if the record is the update's doing - judge by the story, not
  by the nouns.
- **Comeback exception** - all three must hold, or it waits: (1) a genuinely new development, not a new
  angle on the old one (a release date, a delay, a launch, a verdict, a price, a shutdown); (2) the new
  development is bigger than the original story; (3) the edition says plainly that it covered the earlier
  beat, in one clause, so the reader is not sold the same news twice.
- **`memory.py filter` already reports `recent_topics` and `topic_hits`.** That is an input, not a verdict:
  the DB reports, the agent decides (docs/CONTRACTS.md, memory `filter` contract).
- **Never repeat an item at all**, even outside the window, if the key matches - that is a -45 penalty, not
  a judgement call (see SCORING RUBRIC).

## WHAT THE EDITION IS NOT

- Not a news wire. It is a selection with a point of view, and 5 well-chosen stories beat 14 scraped ones.
- Not a reaction compilation. A creator's take is valuable when it adds analysis, worthless when it is
  just a face over the same headline.
- Not a hype sheet. A trailer is evidence of a trailer, not of a good game.
- Not a drama digest. No feuds, no callout threads, no "who is winning the internet today".
- Not a politics page. World news appears only when it materially changes his money, his work or his
  safety (docs/SPEC.md §9).
- Not an AI-hype letter. Model news is reported with the benchmark, the price and the catch.
- Not a spoiler service. He is playing Warframe and building Hytale and Godot projects right now.
- Not a place for a hot take the agent cannot source. One unsourced claim poisons the whole page.
